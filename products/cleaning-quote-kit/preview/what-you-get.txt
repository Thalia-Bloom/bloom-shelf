Post-Construction Cleaning Quote Kit - public sample (not the paid workbook)

You type these inputs on the Quote sheet (green cells). Yellow cells add them up.

  Floor area (sq ft)
  Crew size (people)
  Labor rate you will quote ($ per person-hour)
  Hours you expect each person to work
  Debris level (Light dust / Typical post-construction / Heavy leftover debris)
  Restrooms to detail, plus extra minutes per restroom
  Windows / glass panels, plus extra minutes per window
  Supplies, dumpster / haul-away, travel / parking / after-hours
  Overhead % and uncertainty buffer %

The total is YOUR rate x YOUR hours, plus the extras you typed. It is
not a market rate, not a bid service, and not a published $ / sq ft
table. Bloom does not walk the site.
