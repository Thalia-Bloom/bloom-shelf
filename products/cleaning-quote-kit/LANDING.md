## What it is

You already clean houses. Someone asks you to price a commercial
post-construction job. You need a number you can stand behind, built
from crew size, hours, debris, restrooms, glass, supplies, haul-away,
and the rate you actually charge.

This download is:

- An Excel workbook (Quote sheet, Limits sheet, fictional worked example)
- A site-walk punch list for the first commercial clean
- A short scope-and-exclusions template
- A printable PDF of the text files

You type the green cells. Yellow cells add them up. The total is your
arithmetic, not a published market rate.
