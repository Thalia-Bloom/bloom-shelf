## What it is

A downloadable punch list for a local service-business owner:

- A 0-100 scorecard and rubric for the public listing
- A DIY edit checklist (categories, services, description, attributes,
  hours, photos, posts, Q&A, review-ask)
- A photo plan that insists on photos you have rights to
- SMS and email review-ask templates (honest asks, no fake reviews,
  no gating)
- Eight Q&A drafts and four Google post drafts with blanks for your
  facts
- One fictional worked example so you can see a filled sheet
- A printable PDF of the same worksheets
