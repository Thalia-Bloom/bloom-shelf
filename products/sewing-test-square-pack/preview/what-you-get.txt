Print rule: 100% / actual size. Turn OFF fit-to-page and shrink-to-fit. Measure the printed box with a hard ruler, not the page. If the box is not exact, do not cut fabric.

This is a print-scale check. It is not a sewing pattern, not a tiled-PDF joiner, and not a size chart. No garment fit is guaranteed.
