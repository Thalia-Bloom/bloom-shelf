## How it works

1. Pick Letter or A4 to match the paper in the printer.
2. Pick 4 inch or 10 cm to match what the pattern instructions ask for.
3. Print that one page at 100% / actual size. Turn off fit-to-page.
4. Measure the printed box, not the page.
5. If a 4 inch box measures 3.75 inches, the scale is wrong. Reprint before cutting.
