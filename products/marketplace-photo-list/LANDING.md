## What it is

You already have one object in front of you. You want photos
that show the thing, not a memory of it: all sides, the
underside, the joints, the ding, a tape or a coin for scale,
a label if there is one, the object in place, and a doorway
if it is furniture.

This download is:

- A checkbox shot list for ONE object
- Limits you should read before you treat photos as a listing
- One fictional filled example (Harbor Mill oak sideboard,
  labeled fictional)
- A printable PDF of the same pages

You take the pictures. You check a box only after that photo
is on the phone. You still write your own listing.

## What it is not

- Not a listing bot. It does not post, title, price, or log
  you into a Marketplace account
- Not the Furniture Pickup Go/No-Go Sheet (that is a
  keep-or-walk decision before you haul a piece)
- Not an appraisal and not a market-demand report
- Bloom does not photograph, upload, or sell the object
- No sale, income, or "this will sell" promise

There are no testimonials on this page because we are not
going to invent them.

## Who it is for

Someone listing one object at a time who will stand at that
object and photograph it. If you can shoot all sides, put a
tape or a coin in a frame, and photograph the damage instead
of cropping it out, you can use this list.

## Who it is not for

- Anyone who wants software to post or price the piece
- Anyone who wants a warehouse catalog or a multi-item
  shooting script
- Anyone who will not look at the object in person
- Anyone who wants Bloom to photograph, list, or sell it
