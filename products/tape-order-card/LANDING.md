You already printed tiled A4 or Letter pages at home.
You need the tape order in writing, and you need to measure
the test square before you spend an evening taping the wrong
scale.

This download is:

- A fill-in card: paper match, measure-before-tape, tile count,

  a joined file

  fictional)

You write the pages you actually printed. You measure first.
You tape second. Cutting is last.
