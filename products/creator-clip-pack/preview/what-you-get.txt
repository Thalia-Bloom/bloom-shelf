Creator Clip Shot-List + Caption Pack -- public sample
Worksheets for a recut you make yourself. Not an editor. Not auto-captions.

Shot-list columns you fill for each beat of a source you already have:

  #                    row number
  source in            where the beat starts on the source
  source out           where the beat ends on the source
  keep or cut          KEEP if it belongs in the shorter cut
  picture              what is on screen
  spoken audio         original spoken audio, or none
  on-screen caption    the line that sits on this picture
  notes                title vs speech, and anything you must not forget

One-transcript rule:

  Each published clip gets its own transcript / caption text file.
  Name it source-basename-clip-01.txt (then clip-02 for the next
  upload from the same source). A 12-minute source and a 45-second
  recut are not the same video; do not reuse one transcript for two
  different uploads. This is a filing rule you follow. It is not a
  promise that any platform will display or auto-sync the file.
