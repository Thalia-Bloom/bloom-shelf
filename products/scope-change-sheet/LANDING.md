## What it is

Small jobs get messy when the details live across a text thread, a quick phone call, and memory.

The Handyman Scope + Change Sheet gives you one page for the job you agreed to do and one page for what changed after work started.

## What you receive

- An editable two-page Word file
- A print-ready PDF
- A complete fictional worked example
- A two-page quick-start guide
- Plain-language prompts for scope, exclusions, materials, cleanup, hidden conditions, added time, added price, and approval records

## How it works

1. Fill the Job Scope Sheet before work starts.
2. Name what is included, excluded, and supplied by each person.
3. Record assumptions, cleanup, price reference, and schedule window.
4. If the job changes, pause when practical and fill one Change Note.
5. Record how and when the customer approved, declined, or asked you to pause.
