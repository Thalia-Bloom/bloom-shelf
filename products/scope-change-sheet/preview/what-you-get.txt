Handyman Scope + Change Sheet — public heading sample (not the paid files)

Job Scope Sheet — fill before work starts. One sheet per job.

- Job / record no., customer, property, phone / email
- Job quote / invoice ref., planned / prepared
- 1 Customer request — requested result
- 2 Included work — included task, finish / standard, qty / area
- 3 Materials and exclusions
  - Contractor supplies
  - Customer supplies
  - Not included
  - Access / protection / cleanup
- 4 Assumptions and stop points
- 5 Price, schedule, and review record
  - Price tied to this scope
  - Schedule window
  - Status: approved / declined / paused
  - Method / reference, date / time, names / initials

Change Note — record one addition, discovery, substitution, or removal.

- Job / record no., customer, property, original job
- Scope ref., change no., date / time found
- 1 What changed — change or discovery, reason, photos / files
- 2 Work added, removed, or replaced — scope effect
- 3 Time, materials, price, and schedule
  - Added time / materials
  - Price or billing effect
  - Schedule effect
  - If declined or paused
- 4 Approval record — status, method / reference, date / time
- 5 Field note
