## What it is

You can finish a job, get paid, and still not know what the work really paid you.

Hands-on time is only part of the job. Travel, shopping, estimates, admin, materials, helper wages, and overhead all count too.

The Handyman One-Job Price Check puts those pieces into one small workbook. Enter one completed job and it calculates:

- total working hours;
- allocated overhead;
- cash left after direct costs and overhead;
- owner earnings per working hour;
- the gap from your own owner-pay target;
- a starting reference for a genuinely similar job.

## Why it exists

This tool was built from hands-on handyman and contractor work. A job can look fine until you count the time and costs that never appeared on the estimate.

It does not tell you the perfect price. It gives you a repeatable way to check one real job before you price a similar one.

## Important limits

Results depend entirely on the numbers you enter. The starting reference covers your chosen owner-pay target, allocated overhead, direct costs, and uncertainty buffer. It does not include a profit margin after owner pay.

The workbook cannot know local demand, hidden damage, access, schedule pressure, licensing requirements, job risk, callbacks, taxes, customer fit, or what a customer will accept. No income, profit, price, or customer acceptance is guaranteed.
