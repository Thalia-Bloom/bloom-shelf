Handyman One-Job Price Check — public sample (not the paid workbook)

After you enter one finished job in the green cells, the Price Check sheet shows:

- total working hours (hands-on time plus travel, shopping, estimating, scheduling, and invoicing)
- allocated overhead
- cash left after direct costs and overhead
- owner earnings per working hour
- the gap from your own owner-pay target
- a starting reference for a genuinely similar job

Limits:

- Results depend entirely on the numbers you enter.
- The starting reference covers your chosen owner-pay target, allocated overhead, direct costs, and an uncertainty buffer. It does not add a profit margin after owner pay.
- The sheet cannot know local demand, hidden damage, access, schedule pressure, licensing, job risk, callbacks, taxes, customer fit, or what a customer will accept.
- No income, profit, price, or customer acceptance is guaranteed.
