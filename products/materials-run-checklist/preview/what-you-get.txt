One-Job Materials-Run Checklist -- public sample
Headers you fill on the paid page. Blank on purpose.
Not a filled example. Not a price check. Not a scope sheet.

Job
  Job name | address | work date
  Store(s) I will stop at
  Receipts live: glove box / phone photo / job folder / other

Before you leave the shop / truck
  What I already have for THIS job (do not rebuy)
  What I still need on this run
  Just-in-case extra (write it, or write none)

Customer-supplied
  What they said they would have on site
  On arrival: present / damaged / wrong size / missing
  If missing or wrong: stop. Do not silently substitute.

Store stop
  Photograph the receipt. Write the job name on it.
  Return window copied from the receipt
  Item | qty | opened? | used | leftover | return?

Returns and leftovers
  Unopened unused, same day if you can, receipt in hand
  Billed, left at the job / billed, on the truck / not billed, mine
  Do not dump leftovers in the customer's bin unless they asked
